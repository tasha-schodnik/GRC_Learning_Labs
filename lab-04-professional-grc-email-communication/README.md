# Lab 04 — Professional GRC Stakeholder Communication

## Overview

This lab demonstrates professional stakeholder communication across common Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) scenarios. Using a simulated healthcare environment, I practiced communicating assessment-related information involving evidence collection, control testing, remediation tracking, stakeholder disagreement, and escalation.

The objective was to communicate GRC issues clearly and professionally while maintaining evidence-based conclusions, avoiding unsupported assumptions, and preserving productive working relationships with stakeholders.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Professional GRC stakeholder communication
- Evidence requests and follow-up
- Evaluating evidence sufficiency
- Communicating evidence discrepancies
- Control assessment communication
- Finding and remediation follow-up
- Deadline and remediation tracking
- Stakeholder disagreement management
- Issue escalation
- Evidence-based documentation
- Clear and objective business writing

## Key GRC Concepts Applied

### Evidence Before Conclusion

Assessment conclusions should be based on the applicable requirement and sufficient, reliable evidence. Insufficient evidence does not automatically mean that a control failed.

**Requirement → Evidence → Evaluation → Conclusion**

### Evidence Discrepancies

When evidence conflicts, the discrepancy should be identified and investigated before reaching an assessment conclusion.

**Identify discrepancy → Review facts → Request clarification → Resolve discrepancy → Conclude**

### Remediation Validation

A statement that remediation has been completed is not the same as evidence demonstrating that corrective action was implemented. Supporting evidence should be reviewed before remediation is validated or a finding is closed.

### Professional Disagreement

Stakeholder disagreement does not automatically change an assessment result. Additional information should be evaluated objectively, and conclusions should remain supported by the applicable requirement and available evidence.

### Escalation

Escalation provides visibility and enables appropriate assistance or review when an issue cannot be resolved through normal follow-up. Escalation should remain factual, objective, and focused on the issue rather than the individual.
