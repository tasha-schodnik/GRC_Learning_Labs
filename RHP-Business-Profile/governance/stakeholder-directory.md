# 👥 RHP Stakeholder Directory

## Purpose

This document identifies the primary stakeholders involved in the cybersecurity, privacy, risk, compliance, technology, and business activities of **Raccoon Health & Pharmacy (RHP)**.

The directory establishes a consistent stakeholder structure for RHP cybersecurity and Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) home labs and portfolio projects.

Depending on the activity, stakeholders may serve as system owners, control owners, evidence providers, subject-matter experts, risk owners, remediation owners, policy owners, or approvers.

Because RHP is a small organization, one stakeholder may perform multiple roles.

# 🔍 Common GRC Stakeholder Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | Provides leadership support and helps resolve significant business issues |
| System Owner | Accountable for a system and its business use |
| Process Owner | Responsible for a business or operational process |
| Control Owner | Responsible for implementing or maintaining a control |
| Evidence Provider | Supplies reports, records, screenshots, documentation, or other evidence |
| Subject-Matter Expert (SME) | Provides technical, regulatory, or business expertise |
| Risk Owner | Responsible for managing an identified risk |
| Remediation Owner | Responsible for completing corrective actions |
| Policy Owner | Maintains and reviews an organizational policy |
| Approver | Provides required management authorization |
| GRC / Compliance | Coordinates assessments, documents results, tracks issues, and supports governance |

Because RHP is a small organization, the same individual may perform several of these functions.

# 📞 GRC Communication Paths

Typical RHP assessment interactions may include:

**GRC → System Owner**  
Request system information, control evidence, clarification, or remediation updates.

**GRC → IT / Systems Administrator**  
Request technical evidence, configuration information, logs, account information, or security documentation.

**GRC → HR / Administration**  
Request workforce, onboarding, termination, training, or administrative evidence.

**GRC → Pharmacy Operations**  
Understand operational processes, business impact, system usage, and pharmacy-specific requirements.

**GRC → Compliance & Privacy**  
Clarify regulatory requirements, privacy considerations, policies, compliance documentation, or assessment scope.

**GRC → Leadership**  
Escalate significant risks, findings, exceptions, resource needs, or risk-acceptance decisions.

**GRC → Third Parties**  
Request security documentation, contractual information, assessment evidence, or clarification through appropriate RHP contacts.
